Our lineage

A living tradition since 1888

For 137 years, an unbroken line of Gurus has kept the authentic science of yoga alive, and taught it as a practical, everyday way of living.

Yog Yogeshwar Mahaprabhu Ramlal Ji BhagwanFounder · First Guru Gaddi

  1. 1888Yog Yogeshwar Mahaprabhu Ramlal Ji Bhagwan founds the Ashram, the first Guru Gaddi.
  2. The Guru ParamparaThe teaching passes from Guru to Guru, unbroken, through Yogeshwar DeviDayal Mahamandir in Delhi.
  3. TodayPradhan Yogacharya Swami Amit Dev Ji leads camps across India and abroad.
  4. And now, youEvery camp is free and open to all.
