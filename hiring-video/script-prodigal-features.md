# Prodigal pitch v2 — five features I'd build

For Sangram Raje, Prodigal (Forward Deployed Engineer). About 90 seconds after speed-up.
Simple English, short lines. Read slowly; the voice gets sped up later. Say "Prodigal" slowly.
These are ideas from the outside, not claims about what Prodigal is missing.

---

**Hook**
Hi, I am Unnati. I am an AI engineer.
I studied Prodigal, and I wrote down five features I would build for you.
Each one is something I have already built before.

**Context**
Prodigal's agents talk to borrowers every day.
The next step is agents that don't just talk. They act: they take payments, read documents, and follow each lender's rules.

**Feature one — Pay on the call**
The agent sends a secure payment link while the borrower is still on the call.
It sees the payment land, updates the balance, and confirms right away.
I built payment integrations at Paysecure, for forty merchants on Shopify, BigCommerce and WooCommerce.

**Feature two — Read the proof**
A borrower says "I already paid" and uploads a receipt. Or sends a hardship letter.
The agent reads it, checks it against the ledger, and moves the account to the right next step.
At Zomato, I moved invoice reading to our own OCR system, and the cost per invoice went from thirty five rupees to five.

**Feature three — Each lender's rulebook**
Every lender has different rules for settlements and hardship plans.
The agent looks up that lender's policy and answers with the exact rule it used.
At Grid Dynamics, I built RAG pipelines with eighty percent retrieval accuracy.

**Feature four — Launch a new lender in days**
A screen where the ops team can build and test a new call flow without waiting for engineers.
At Grid Dynamics, I built a scenario builder that three to four hundred developers use.

**Feature five — Call Guard**
Simple rules that check every reply before the borrower hears it.
In my demo, the agent alone passes four out of twelve test calls. With Call Guard, all twelve pass.
At Zomato, I built the evaluation framework for GenAI bots used by every app user.

A Forward Deployed Engineer sits with the customer and ships what they need.
That is what I do for VISA today.
