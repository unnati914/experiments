# Pramaana Labs pitch: script

Say "Pramaana" as **pra-MAA-na**. Read at your normal pace, and I'll speed it up later.
If you stumble, pause and say the whole line again. I'll cut out the bad take.

1. Hi, I'm Unnati. I'm an AI engineer. I built something small for Pramaana Labs.

2. Pramaana wants AI that does not make things up. Every answer comes with a proof.

3. I love this idea. So I built a tiny version of it, on Indian tax rules.

4. Here is why it matters. Say your salary is twelve lakh, eighty-five thousand.

5. A normal chatbot may say your tax is about sixty-four thousand rupees.

6. But the real answer is ten thousand, four hundred. Because of a rule called marginal relief.

7. My demo checks every step against a written rule. And it shows you each step.

8. If one fact is missing, like your age, it asks you. It does not guess.

9. And if the question is outside its rules, it says no.

10. I wrote sixteen test cases. Limits, exceptions, rules that clash, missing facts, broken input, and a document that tries to trick the AI. All sixteen pass.

11. I also found four underrated features to build on top. One: if a fact is missing, ask one question instead of saying no. Two: a report that shows which answers change when the law changes. Three: tests written straight from the rules, at every limit. Four: a simple proof receipt that a CA can sign.

12. And the hard part. A proof is only as good as the rule you wrote. So I would write each rule two ways, and send any mismatch to a tax expert.

13. Before this, I built evals for GenAI bots at Zomato, payment systems at Paysecure, and GenAI agents for VISA at Grid Dynamics.

14. I'm applying for the Software Engineer, Product role. I would love to help Pramaana make AI answers you can trust. The link is below. Thank you!
