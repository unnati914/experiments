# Final script: "A builder's story" (cinematic, ~3:00)

How to read it: slow, calm, a little lower than your normal voice, like you're telling a story at night.
"…" = a full 1–2 second pause. Don't rush them; the silence is what makes it cinematic.
Leave a 2-second gap between sections. Hindi words are welcome wherever they feel natural.
[Brackets] = what's on screen; don't read them.

---

## Cold open (0:00–0:12)
[Black screen. A single cursor blinks. Typing sounds. Slow push-in.]

"Every engineer has a resume. …
This isn't mine. …
This is what I build."

[Title card, letterboxed: UNNATI CHHABRA — a builder's story]

## Act 1: The beginning (0:12–0:30)
[Laptop glow in the dark, 2:07 AM clock, hackathon badges stacking up]

"It started in college. At two in the morning. …
A hackathon. Then another. Then another.
I didn't just want to learn to code. I wanted to build things people actually use. …
So I started CodXCrypt, a community of people who felt the same way."

## Act 2: Open source (0:30–0:52)
[GitHub contribution graph fills up green. Counters: 171 repos · 115 merged PRs. Logos drift past: Meshery · Layer5 · Keploy · Open Horizon · Agno · MLH]

"Then I found open source. …
I built Care4ther, an accident-awareness app. Sixty-eight stars, twenty forks, and I mentored around fifty students on it.
Since then … a hundred and seventy-one repos. And over a hundred merged pull requests into other people's projects.
Meshery. Layer5. Keploy. Open Horizon at the Linux Foundation. And Agno, an AI agent framework."

## Act 3: The stage (0:52–1:08)
[Stage lights. A mic. Workshop rooms filling up. Badges: GitHub Campus Expert · Microsoft Learn Student Ambassador · LFX Mentee · MLH]

"Before Zomato … I was in DevRel.
On stages, running workshops, teaching developers how to build.
GitHub Campus Expert. Microsoft Student Ambassador. LFX mentee. …
That's where I learned the other half of building. … Explaining it."

## Act 4: The break (1:08–1:28)
[Music drops out. Silence. Then: 🏆 → Zomato logo fades in, slow and big]

"Then Zomato ran a hackathon. …
I won. …
And that's how I walked into Zomato.

Orders at Zomato. Payments at Paysecure. Gen AI for VISA at Grid Dynamics.
Different problems. … Same question, every single time."

## Act 5: The product person (1:28–1:40)
[Two questions appear one at a time, centered, huge type]

"Who is this for? …
And do they actually want it? …
Because working … is not the same as wanted."

## Act 6: On AI (1:40–1:55)
[Slow reveal of an eval loop, glowing lines tracing between boxes]

"Everyone can demo AI now. …
Very few can make you trust it. …
So I measure everything. Evals before vibes.
And when the model gets it wrong, I check what we gave it first."

## Act 7: After hours (1:55–2:18)
[Repo cards drift in like polaroids on a desk: hirvana · Resume-Roaster · job-hunt-agent · ai-outreach-agent · resume-optimiser-agent · OopsBot · CrushMeter-X]

"After hours … I keep building.
Hirvana, to help people get hired.
Resume Roaster, for brutally honest feedback. Imlea, for kids learning their curriculum.
A job-hunt agent. An outreach agent. A resume-optimiser agent. …
I have built more AI agents than I've had hot meals.
And OopsBot … for the texts you forgot to reply to."

## Act 8: The mindset (2:18–2:30)
[A folder labelled "failed" opens: x-dating and friends fan out; text: shipping > perfect]

"I keep a folder of everything that failed. …
It's my favourite folder. …
Because shipping beats perfect. And every flop got me here faster."

## Act 9: Why me (2:30–2:50)
[Black screen. One line at a time, big and slow: owns the problem · talks to users · designs the system · builds the AI · ships the UI · explains it on stage]

"So … why should you hire me? …
Because I don't just write code. I own the problem.
I can talk to users, design the system, build the AI, ship the frontend …
and then stand on a stage and explain why it matters. …
I move fast. I measure what I ship. And I stay curious when things break."

## Finale (2:50–3:06)
[Music swells. Slow pull-out. End card: unnati-chhabra.xyz · github.com/unnati914 · open to work]

"So here I am. … Still building. …
Looking for the next team to build with.
Early-stage, mid-size, or an MNC. Full-time.
If that's you … let's build something. …

And please … don't ghost me."
