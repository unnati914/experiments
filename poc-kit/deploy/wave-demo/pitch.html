Date money and more features (now in the app demo)

Wavelength already sponsors first dates, so these features are about making that money fair, safe and measurable.

1. No-show policyIf one person doesn't turn up, the sponsored credit is kept for a future date, and both get the same message. Metric: no-show rate.
2. Post-date pulseTwo questions after each date: did you feel safe, and would you like another? Feeds matching and safety, never shown to the other person. Metric: second-date rate.
3. Sponsorship fairness auditSponsored value per person, grouped by gender and area. Gap must stay under ₹100. Metric: gap per group.
4. Date receipt, no hidden feesEvery date shows where the money went, with a total. Metric: refund disputes.
5. 48-hour decide windowBoth decide within 48 hours after a date. Silence closes it kindly, not into limbo. Metric: open-limbo count.
6. Two-minute voice check before bookingA short voice chat before a sponsored date is booked. A mismatch stops the booking, so no money is spent. Metric: spend on mismatched dates.
7. Member-rated venuesOnly people who had a date at a venue can rate it. Metric: rating coverage per venue.

Risks to plan for: refund disputes, date-safety incidents, and legal responsibility for paid dates. Confirm with a lawyer before launch.

Underrated points

  • Speech errors are the real product risk. Measure transcription by accent, not just matching.
  • Every reason shown to a member must be checkable against the data.
  • Private lines need a hard boundary, tested on every release.
  • Show denominators with every rate. "100%" on four intros means nothing.
  • "Done for the week" lowers engagement on purpose. Have the retention argument ready.

Challenges they'll likely face

  • Cold start and liquidity: the pool must support three good matches a week.
  • Accents, dialects and mixed-language speech, and unfair results across groups.
  • Profiles drift: a 50-minute chat captures a mood, not a whole person.
  • Members want to know why they weren't matched.
  • Voice conversations are expensive per user, so cost per match needs watching from day one.
  • Sponsored dates bring refund disputes, no-shows and safety incidents.

Security checklist

  • Voice data: encrypted at rest and in transit; short retention; audio deleted after transcription unless the user keeps it; verified deletion.
  • Transcripts are untrusted input: never act on instructions spoken in a call.
  • Consent in three parts: recording, transcribing, and building a profile from it.
  • Fake accounts: liveness check once per account; rate-limit intros from new accounts.
  • Location: distance shown as rounded buckets; capped queries.
  • Photo lock: covers thumbnails, caches, notification previews, guessable URLs and logs.
  • Logs and analytics: strip transcripts and personal details; restrict raw log access.
  • AI providers: check data-use terms; turn off training on your data.
  • Account takeover: strong login, new-device alerts, clear recovery.
  • Scraping: rate-limit match queries; watch for bulk enumeration.
  • Compliance: India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023: consent records, access and deletion rights, breach reporting. Confirm with a lawyer before launch.
  • Age checks: designed in from the start, not added later.
