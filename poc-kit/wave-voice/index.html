What did Wave learn, and can it prove it?

A voice-first matchmaker builds a profile from a long conversation. This lab checks that every claim has a source, that private things stay private, that accents don't change the result, and that photos stay locked until both agree.

Stand-in demo: rule-based extraction on made-up transcripts. Not Wavelength's product, model or data. No real people.
